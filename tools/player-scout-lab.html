🎲 Season Simulator

Simulating…
Actually simulates matches, not just a formula: for every simulated match each player takes a random number of shots and fouls (drawn from a Poisson distribution scaled by their position and ratings), each shot converts to a goal at a rate driven by their Goal Threat, and every foul runs through the same yellow/straight-red logic as engine/referee.js (aggression-driven, escalating with repeat fouls in a match). The season is replayed many times so you get a realistic mean and a 10th–90th percentile range rather than one lucky/unlucky roll.
How these scores are calculated

These aren't made up. Every rating below is derived from the same formulas your match engine actually runs during simulation (see engine/shooting.js, engine/defending.js, engine/goalkeeper.js), not a separate guess. Attributes are curved the same way the engine curves them — a jump from 80→90 in a stat matters a lot more than 50→60 — and the same skill/playstyle bonuses the engine applies mid-match are applied here.

Goal Threat — Finishing (primary), Off-the-Ball Awareness (getting into a shooting position), Kicking Power, and Heading, plus bonuses for finishing-flavoured skills (Phenomenal Finishing, Acrobatic Finishing, Blitz Curler…) and box-focused playstyles (Goal Poacher, Fox in the Box…).

Passing — Low Pass, Lofted Pass, Ball Control and Off-the-Ball Awareness (reading where a pass needs to go).

Dribbling — Dribbling, Ball Control, Balance, Acceleration and Tight Possession.

Defending — Tackling and Defensive Awareness (primary), Defensive Engagement and Aggression, plus bonuses for tackling skills (Sliding Tackle, Interception, Man Marking…) and defensive playstyles (Destroyer, Anchor Man…).

Physical — Speed, Acceleration, Jumping and Physical Condition.

Shot-Stopping — only meaningful for goalkeepers: GK Reflexes, GK Catching, GK Parrying, GK Reach and GK Awareness. Outfield players are floored low here on purpose.

Discipline (higher = more disciplined) is 100 − Foul Risk, where Foul Risk comes straight from the match engine's foulProneness() function: driven mainly by Aggression, offset by Defensive Awareness, with a smaller push from Physical Condition. A player at 50 is exactly the engine's neutral baseline (multiplier 1.0×); below 50 means the engine treats them as more likely to give away fouls and pick up cards than average.

Data quality: Full means the player has a matching entry in player-attributes.json and every score above is formula-accurate. Basic means only teams.json's five headline ratings (ATT/DEF/PHY/PAC/TEC) were available, so the scores are a reasonable estimate rather than the real engine calculation — load or fill in that player's attribute sheet in the Attributes Editor tool for a precise read.
