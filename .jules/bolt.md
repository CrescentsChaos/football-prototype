## 2025-05-18 - Hoisting Loop Invariants in Decision Engine
**Learning:** In `evaluateBallActions()`, candidate actions were evaluated inside a loop over up to 9 possible ball actions (`pass`, `dribble`, `carry`, `shoot`, `cross`, `throughball`, `backpass`, `switch`, `hold`). Operations such as `playerSideData(player)` (squad array lookups), `halfSpaceOf(player)` (positioning geometry), `localOverload(...)` (spatial presence across all outfield players), and `personality.includes(...)` were being computed on every iteration. Hoisting these loop invariants out of the per-action loop eliminates redundant array searches and spatial calculations per ball decision across match simulation hot loops.
**Action:** Always check candidate/action evaluation loops in simulation engines for contextual lookups and spatial calculations that depend only on the player/entity rather than the candidate action itself, and hoist them above the loop.
