## 2025-02-18 - Hoisting Loop Invariants in Decision Engine Hot Loops
**Learning:** In `evaluateBallActions()`, spatial calculations (`localOverload`), formation lookups (`halfSpaceOf`), squad searches (`playerSideData`), and attribute/skill reads (`hasSkill`, `xattr`) were executed per candidate ball action inside an `allowed.forEach` loop (9 actions per ball decision). Because these context calculations depend solely on the player, pitch zone, and match state rather than individual candidate actions, executing them inside the loop multiplied expensive squad array filtering and spatial presence calculations by 9x on every decision tick across every match simulation.
**Action:** Always inspect decision evaluation functions for loop-invariant context and spatial calculations that can be hoisted above candidate loops in simulation hot loops.
