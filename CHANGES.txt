WHAT'S NEW
 - Accounts remember earlier seasons: every finished season / awards round / league / cup / World Cup /
   Champions League run is snapshotted (top performers' lines) into the media save (apexMedia -> sx.lg).
   Honours are read retroactively from the trophy case. Posts and replies can now cite last season, season
   trails ("Y1 30G/9A - Y2 34G/9A"), career totals, honours, records held and the last tournament.
   Profile cards show market value, form factor, reputation, honours, records and a season-by-season table.
 - Records & leaderboards: top 5 ever per competition family and scope (single season / single league season /
   single tournament) for goals, assists, G+A, MOTM, clean sheets, saves, average rating. New Media tabs:
   "Records" (record books + break log) and "Hall of Fame" (all-time career leaders, most decorated, most valuable).
 - Record-breaking is featured: live while a season/tournament runs (past the all-time mark) and when it is
   archived (final tally, previous holder reacts). News outlets, stats account, the player, the club and fans all post;
   feed filter "Records & milestones". Career milestones (50/100/... goals, assists, MOTM, clean sheets), throwbacks,
   season/tournament recaps and "Nth award" stories also use the history.
 - Rating no longer decides reputation: followers, verified accounts, who posts/replies, rumour subjects now come from
   reputation points (output, this season's form, honours, records). The overall rating survives only as a small prior
   that fades with minutes played (cfg.rep.prior) - set prior.max to 0 to remove it entirely.
 - Performance feeds the transfer fee: market value = ability x recent performance (recency-weighted over 3 seasons,
   position-scaled) x honours/records premium. Rumour fees use it (seller fame / talk stage nudge the price).
 - Config: media.json -> SOCIAL.cfg.{rep, legacy, value} and new banks (md_record_*, pp_record_*, pp_milestone, ...).
   verify.playerMinOvr was replaced by verify.minRep. Old saves load fine; the ledger fills from the next archived
   season/tournament. "Clear media" keeps the ledger; "Reset EVERYTHING" wipes it.
 - Tests: node test/legacy-harness.js (no jsdom needed). The older jsdom harnesses were not runnable here.

WHAT'S NEW
 - Verified accounts: every club has an official account; every player rated at or above
   SOCIAL.cfg.verify.playerMinOvr has a verified account (handle, followers from rating,
   club fame, career stats, trophies/awards and current form). Click a name in the feed for
   a profile card (followers, fanbase name/tier, fan mood).
 - They post and talk: clubs post results/previews/trophies, players react to goals, hat-tricks,
   winners, MOTM, saves, red cards, injuries, derbies, trophies, rumours and awards. Posts get
   threads - teammates, the club, an opposition player, fans of the player, rival-fan trolls,
   and the author replying back. Media outlets and clubs quote-post big moments.
 - Fanbases: each player has a named fanbase (e.g. "Salah Army") sized from followers, with a
   tier and a mood ("heat") that rises with goals/MOTM/trophies and falls with reds and losses.
 - Awards hype BEFORE the announcement: a rolling race update every N matches, a shortlist once
   the season is in its closing rounds, and a full "eve" build-up (breaking news, TV special with
   pundit picks, odds, polls, articles, tabloid gossip, nominees' own posts, club and fan
   campaigns) right before awards are handed out - for the season awards and for tournament
   awards (Golden Boot/Ball/Glove). After the reveal the winner, club, losing nominees and fans
   react and the media marks its own predictions (called it / upset / shock).
 - Debates: fanbase-vs-fanbase wars (award races, striker showdowns, "greatest of the era",
   title talk, derbies) with a poll weighted by form AND fanbase size, a fan-war thread, player
   cameos by persona, escalations, and a verdict when the award decides it.
 - New Media tabs: Awards Watch (live leaders + odds), Debates, Accounts. New feed filters:
   Players & clubs, Awards hype.

NO HARD-CODING: all wording, thresholds, weights, handle/fan-name patterns, the award list and
the outlet "recipes" for each hype phase live in media.json -> SOCIAL (cfg + banks). Add awards,
tune posting rates, rename fanbases or add lines without touching code.
Save-safe: media never uses the seeded RNG; state lives in the existing "apexMedia" key
(older saves load fine); post caps are configurable (SOCIAL.cfg.caps).
Tests: node test/social-harness.js. (end-season, league-tournament and roles harnesses already
failed on the previous zip - Golden Boot / 8-club matchday count / penalty-icon expectations.)

APEX MEDIA CENTRE - news channels, TV studio, pundits and social media.

New tab "📡 Media" with four views: Feed (X-style timeline with reply threads,
polls, score cards, article and video cards), News (tabloid / broadsheet
article cards), TV Studio (live banner, anchor script, 3-pundit panel, scrolling
score ticker) and Trending (hashtags + outlets). Filter by team or post type.

WHAT GETS COVERED
 - Every finished match (live or auto-simmed): scoreline, breaking news, club
   desks, 5-7 fan reactions, memes, radio rants, video titles, stats, tactics,
   VAR/red-card posts, injury news, Man-of-the-Match poll, TV bulletin with
   pundits, broadsheet + tabloid articles. Coverage is tiered by newsworthiness
   (derbies, finals, upsets, comebacks, late winners, hat-tricks, red cards,
   streaks, your career club...) so big games are loud and routine ones quiet.
 - Match hype: preview posts for upcoming fixtures after each matchday and the
   moment you kick off a live match (form, stakes, key men, injuries, win-
   probability model, pundit predictions, referee, fan predictions, polls).
   A "Pre-match buzz" card shows above the pitch; a "Media reaction" card shows
   after full time.
 - World events: trophies and awards, season previews/reviews, title race and
   relegation battle updates, team of the week, top scorers, goal milestones,
   sack-watch after losing streaks, injury crises, transfer rumours with an
   insider "interest -> talks -> HERE WE GO" ladder, transfer-window and
   deadline-day chatter, monthly calendar flavour, debate polls.

WHY IT FEELS INFINITE
 ~2,000 template lines with nested {?a|b|c} choices and [bank] references,
 filled from real data (scorers, minutes, xG, cards, form, h2h, tables), 20
 storylines, ~12 outlet voices, 7 pundit lenses, 8 fan moods, and fan accounts
 minted deterministically per club (the same regulars keep turning up). An
 anti-repeat memory stops lines recurring.

SAFE FOR YOUR SAVES
 - Media never touches seededRandom(), so seeded matches/seasons are unchanged.
 - Stored under the single key "apexMedia" (capped ~170 KB), included in
   Export/Import, wiped by Reset. "Clear feed" button in the tab.
 - All outlets/pundits are FICTIONAL. Rename them in data/mediaData.js.
 - Add derbies in MEDIA_DERBIES (data/mediaData.js).
